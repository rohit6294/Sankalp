About Us

Establishment: 2026

Our Mission

We are not here to sell another "guaranteed rank" promise, repackage standard books in plastic wrap, or push you into yet another coaching factory. We are here to teach — patiently, repeatedly, in a way that respects how learning actually works.

Sankalp was founded in 2026 to help West Bengal engineering aspirants crack the WBJEE without commercial theatre. We focus on providing high-quality study materials, honest practice, and direct mentor support. Many students in Bengal are forced to choose between massive coaching centers that teach in languages they aren't fully comfortable with, or outdated tuition classes. Sankalp bridges this gap with clear, focused, English-Bengali guided modules.

What We Provide

1. Interactive Study Modules

No more boring paragraphs of pure theory. We break down physics, chemistry, and mathematics concepts through concise sheets, worked diagrams, and instant quick tests.

2. Real WBJEE Pattern Mock Tests

Practice under pressure with Category 1, 2, and 3 questions. Read detailed worked solutions and explanation keys compiled by expert teachers right after submission.

3. Direct 1-on-1 Mentoring

Every student has access to schedule doubt-clearing sessions or customized strategy counselling. We talk to you directly to guide you through your prep challenges.

4. Verified Cutoff Tools

Compare engineering branches and institutions across West Bengal using official round 1 and round 2 historical cutoff ranges, helping you choose the right college during counselling.
