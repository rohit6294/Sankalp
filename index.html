Frequently Asked Questions

Common doubts about WBJEE 2026 marking, cutoffs, and mentorship answered honestly.

How does Category 3 partial marking work in WBJEE?
Category 3 questions have multiple correct options. If you mark all correct options, you receive +2 marks. If you mark any incorrect option, you get strictly 0. However, if you mark a subset of correct options with zero incorrect selections, you receive partial credit calculated as: 2 × (Correct Options Marked ÷ Total Actual Correct Options). Our Marks Calculator computes this automatically!
Is Jadavpur University 90% reserved for Home State students?
Yes, under the West Bengal Domicile policy, 90% of general seats in state government universities and colleges (including Jadavpur University) are reserved for candidates who fulfill the WB Domicile criteria (A1, A2, or B certificates). The remaining 10% seats are open to All India quota candidates.
What is TFW (Tuition Fee Waiver) and who is eligible?
The Tuition Fee Waiver (TFW) scheme exempts eligible students from paying tuition fees in participating engineering colleges. To be eligible, your annual family income must be under ₹2.5 Lakhs, and you must hold a valid WB domicile and an official Income Certificate issued by a competent authority.
How does the Sankalp College Predictor calculate admission chances?
Our predictor evaluates over 3,300+ official Opening & Closing Rank (ORCR) records from Round 1 and Round 2 across 87+ engineering institutions. It categorizes your admission probability into High, Medium, and Low chances based on historical cutoff margins and reservation categories.
What is 1-on-1 Counselling and how does it work?
1-on-1 Counselling is a dedicated 20-30 minute private video session on Google Meet with senior engineers from Jadavpur University. You can discuss your rank, college choices, branch interest vs placement tradeoffs, and get your choice-filling sequence reviewed before final lock-in.
