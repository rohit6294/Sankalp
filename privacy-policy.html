Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Sankalp Aspirants ("we," "our," "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our users ("you," "student," "aspirant"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website sankalp-learning-5442f.web.app, utilize our online tools, or enroll in our prep courses.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information about you in the following ways:

  • Personal Details: Name, email address, password, phone number, state, and WBJEE exam year targeting when you register an account.
  • Educational Data: Student exam answers, mock test scores, diagnostic logs, and college preference metrics calculated through our tools.
  • Payment Info: Transactions are processed via Razorpay. We do not store credit card/UPI details on our servers; we only store transaction completion status and payment IDs.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, and usage patterns collected via Firebase Analytics.

3. Cookies & Advertising Policies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance user experiences on our platform. In addition, we cooperate with third-party networks:

  • Google AdSense: We may display Google AdSense advertisements on our public resources. Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads to users based on their visits to our site and other sites on the internet.
  • Opt-Out: Users may opt-out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings page or utilizing browser extensions.

4. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected information to:

  • Manage accounts and provide personalized exam analysis.
  • Generate Jadavpur University and government engineering college cutoff probability predictions.
  • Process payments securely and verify course subscriptions.
  • Distribute important study notices or response keys via email automations.

5. Data Protection & Deletion Rights

All data is stored securely using Google Firebase/Firestore infrastructures. Under applicable data protection laws, you have the right to access your stored data, request corrections, or request complete account/data deletion. To request deletion, please contact us at the email address listed in our contact page.

6. Policy Modifications

We reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated modification date.
