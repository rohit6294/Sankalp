How the WBJEE College Predictor Works

During the WBJEE counselling process (administered by the WBJEEB board), engineering colleges are allotted to students strictly based on their General Merit Rank (GMR) and Category ranks.

The cutoff rank for any particular stream at an institute represents the rank of the last student who secured admission in that year. Our predictive algorithm uses historical Round 1 and Round 2 cutoff limits to determine your probability index:

  • High Chance (Mint Green): Your entered GMR rank is comfortably below the historical cutoff closing limit (specifically within 90% of the closing cutoff). This suggests a very safe probability of admission.
  • Medium Chance (Gold): Your entered GMR rank is extremely close to the historical closing cutoff boundaries (between 90% and 110% of the closing cutoff). Admission in Round 1 is competitive, but holds strong potential in Round 2.
  • Low Chance (Red): Your GMR rank is slightly above the historical cutoff (between 110% and 130% of the closing cutoff). Admission is highly competitive and depends entirely on vacancies or mop-up rounds.

Target Jadavpur University Cutoffs 2026

Jadavpur University is the premier destination for WBJEE candidates. It is consistently ranked among the top engineering universities in India. Because of its extremely low tuition fees (around ₹10,000 for 4 years) and excellent placements, its cutoffs close exceptionally early.

For Unreserved Home State (General Bengal) candidates, here are the historical cutoff closing trends for top branches:

  • Computer Science & Engineering (CSE): Closes under GMR 90. Highly competitive.
  • Information Technology (IT): Closes under GMR 280.
  • Electronics & Telecommunication (ETCE): Closes under GMR 390.
  • Electrical Engineering (EE): Closes under GMR 980.
  • Mechanical Engineering (ME): Closes under GMR 1,400.
  • Chemical Engineering: Closes under GMR 2,100.
  • Civil Engineering: Closes under GMR 3,200.
