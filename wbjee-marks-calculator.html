How the WBJEE Marks Calculator Works

The West Bengal Joint Entrance Examination (WBJEE) is renowned for its highly technical three-tier scoring structure. Standard entrance tests rely on a single uniform negative marking system, but WBJEE divides its scoring architecture into three distinct categories across Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry.

Here is the breakdown of Category rules that our calculator evaluates programmatically:

  • Category 1: Correct choices receive +1 mark. Each incorrect choice incurs a penalty of -0.25 marks. Skipped questions award 0.
  • Category 2: Correct choices receive +2 marks. Each incorrect choice incurs a penalty of -0.5 marks. Skipped questions award 0.
  • Category 3 (Multiple-Choice Select): Programmatically challenging. Questions feature one or more correct options. Marking all correct options awards +2 marks. If you mark any incorrect option, your score for that question is strictly 0. However, if you mark a subset of correct options without marking any incorrect ones, you receive partial credit:
    Partial Score = 2 × (Number of Correct Options Marked / Total Actual Correct Options)
